## PROJECT

Create a world-class, premium corporate website for **MAP EDITION GROUP LIMITED**, a Ghanaian diversified company operating across technology, transportation, equipment, supplies, and business solutions.

MAP Edition Group should feel like the digital headquarters of a serious, established, highly capable African corporation — **premium, trustworthy, modern, intelligent, powerful, and exceptionally easy to navigate**.

The website must transform MAP Edition Group from a traditional company-profile presentation into a **modern corporate digital experience designed to win contracts, attract corporate clients, generate enquiries, showcase capabilities, and establish immediate credibility.**

The most important principle: **TRUST BEFORE PRODUCTS.**

Every section must drive credibility and enquiry. Do NOT make the website complicated. Do NOT make it feel like a generic WordPress template. Do NOT overload visitors with walls of text, popups, fake statistics, or unnecessary information.

---

## IMPORTANT BRAND ASSET

Use the official **MAP Edition Group logo** as the central visual reference throughout the website.

**Do NOT:**
- Redesign, recolour, distort, crop, or replace the logo
- Alter the logo's navy/black/blue/cyan identity
- Substitute placeholder logos in production

Preserve the logo's professional corporate appearance across all pages, navigation, footer, and mobile views.

---

## NON-NEGOTIABLES

**Do NOT invent:**
- Vehicle models, rental prices, or availability
- Lenovo product SKUs, pricing, or warranty terms
- Partnerships not verified in the company profile
- Client projects or case studies
- Testimonials or review quotes
- Statistics, revenue figures, or employee counts
- Social media accounts that do not exist

**Do NOT expose:**
- Private reference phone numbers of client contacts on the public website

**Do NOT simulate:**
- Successful form submissions, quote confirmations, or payment flows without real backend integration

If a backend, CMS, or payment system is unavailable during development, clearly structure integration points for later connection. Never pretend functionality succeeded.

# 2. VISUAL DIRECTION

Create a **premium African corporate aesthetic** rather than a basic template.

### Overall Style

- Luxury corporate
- Modern technology company
- Executive-level presentation
- Clean and spacious
- High-end B2B
- Subtle futuristic elements
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Professional photography
- Sophisticated micro-interactions

The website should look expensive without becoming complicated. Use generous whitespace, cinematic corporate photography, elegant cards, strong typography, subtle shadows, and clear visual hierarchy.

### Color Direction

Build the visual identity around the existing MAP Edition Group logo.

**Primary palette:**
- Deep navy / midnight blue
- Black
- Clean white
- Electric/corporate blue
- Subtle cyan accents
- Very light silver/grey backgrounds

Use blue/cyan accents strategically to connect the website with the company's technology and Lenovo distribution activities.

**Avoid:** Excessive gradients, excessive glassmorphism, cartoon graphics, flashy startup aesthetics, cheap-looking colour combinations.

The result should look closer to a **major international corporate group** than a small local business website.

### Typography

- Oversized editorial headings for hero and section titles
- Clean sans-serif body text (e.g. Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or similar)
- Strong weight contrast between headings and body
- Generous line-height for readability
- Short paragraphs — never walls of text
- Elegant numerical displays for statistics (CMS-managed only)
